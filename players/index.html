Player questions.

Can I score padel matches on Apple Watch?

Yes. PadelWrist supports Standard Match and Fixed Points scoring on Apple Watch, with a companion experience on iPhone and iPad.

Will PadelWrist save my match history and stats?

Yes. Saved matches feed searchable History and Insights, including recent form, partner and opponent records and performance trends based on recorded results.

Can I repeat a match with the same players and settings?

Yes. PadelWrist 1.9 adds Play Again from Match History on iPhone and iPad. Review the previous setup and start a separate new match, or prepare for Watch from iPhone.

Can clubs use PadelWrist for tournaments?

The released app can score individual Fixed Points matches. PadelWrist Events is in development for clubs and tournament hosts and does not yet manage public event draws or standings.
