Reference

The PadelWrist padel glossary is the cross-reference layer for scoring, walls, tactics, shots, equipment and social-format terminology. If a term in one of these guides is unfamiliar, the glossary should be the quickest route back into the topic.

How we write these guides

Rules-based pages use FIP as the primary source and display their review date. Tactical and technique pages explain widely used padel principles without pretending a webpage replaces a coach. Social formats are described as common formats rather than universal regulations.
