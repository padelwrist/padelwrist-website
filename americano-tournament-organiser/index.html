How to organise an Americano padel tournament.

An Americano is a great way to get a club group playing with different people. A little preparation makes the rotations fairer and the evening easier to run.

1. Choose the number of players and courts

Padel doubles uses four players per court. With 12 players and three courts you can run three matches simultaneously; with 10 players and two courts, two people must rest each round. Decide the court capacity and rest policy before generating fixtures. A roster that fits your available time matters more than making the schedule look perfectly symmetrical.

If your group has a mix of abilities, explain whether the goal is social mixing or competitive balance. Americano usually favours the former.

2. Agree your Americano scoring rules

Many Americanos use a fixed total of rally points per court rather than normal games and sets. With a 24-point target, a match might finish 14–10 or 12–12. Each player receives their pair's points for the round. For example, a player on the side that scores 14 points receives 14 towards their individual standing.

There is no single prescribed points target for every club. Choose 16, 24, 32 or another suitable total and be consistent throughout the event. Agree the treatment of tied totals and interrupted matches before starting.

3. Create partner rotations

The appeal of Americano is changing partners and opponents across rounds. A good draw spreads those combinations while avoiding unnecessary repeated pairings. Once the roster, number of courts and points target are set, PadelWrist Events can prepare proposed draws. The organiser reviews assignments before publishing a round.

Consider putting a visible court number next to each fixture, particularly when several matches begin together. When players know their partner, opponents and court in advance, the transition to the next round takes far less explanation.

4. Decide how rests affect the leaderboard

If not everyone can play in every round, resting points change the final rankings. PadelWrist 2.0 offers the organiser a choice between a personal-average points credit for rests and no rest points. Neither should be presented as the one official Americano convention. Choose a rule that suits the group, explain it and keep it consistent.

If someone withdraws, confirm how their previous results remain in the record and how the remaining rounds will work. A transparent rule is more important than a perfectly balanced-looking table.

5. Record scores and show individual standings

After each match, record the actual points won by both pairs. Players then accumulate their credited points across the rounds. A narrow defeat still contributes points, which is why Americano rewards every rally and not only match wins.

In PadelWrist Events, the organiser manages official scores and corrections. Participants can follow the published rounds, court assignments, results and standings with a read-only event code or link. At the end, review the ranking and podium together.

A simple pre-event checklist

  • Confirmed player list and available courts
  • Chosen point target and approximate court time
  • Published scoring, tie and rest rules
  • Reviewed partner rotations and court assignments
  • One person responsible for official results and corrections
  • A way to share each round and the final standings
