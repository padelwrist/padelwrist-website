# Original PadelWrist category artwork

These ten vector illustrations were authored for PadelWrist in this repository on 1 November 2026. They contain original geometric equipment/court motifs, PadelWrist colours and no product photographs, manufacturer logos or Amazon content. They are category illustrations, not depictions of a particular product or endorsement.

Sources: `catalogue/images/category-*.svg`. PadelWrist may use the artwork on padelwrist.com and in its iOS/iPadOS application. Native PNG representations are rendered from these originals; Apple SF Symbols are not claimed as PadelWrist-authored imagery.

The shared source catalogue records each URL, source type, evidence reference, review status and permitted surfaces. Manufacturer-supplied photographs require their own written permission covering website and app use; this record cannot authorise them. Amazon Creators API images are also outside this record and must never be downloaded or rehosted as editorial imagery.
