Knowledge base standards

How PadelWrist writes about padel.

The knowledge base exists to answer real player questions accurately and clearly. It is not a vehicle for publishing hundreds of near-identical search pages.

Primary sources first

For official rules, our primary authority is the International Padel Federation (FIP). Where UK context is useful, we may also use the LTA. Competition-specific rules are identified as such rather than presented as universal rules.

Rules and habits are not the same thing

Padel has many club conventions and social formats. We distinguish an official rule from a common playing habit. Americano and Mexicano, for example, vary by organiser, so we describe common structures and explicitly say where there is no single universal rule set.

Review dates mean something

A review date is updated when the article has actually been checked against its relevant sources or materially edited. We do not refresh dates simply to make old content appear new.

Product comparisons

PadelWrist is a product in the scoring-app category, so competitor comparisons cannot be presented as independent journalism. We disclose that conflict, use current first-party product listings where possible and describe products by use case rather than declaring ourselves the winner.

Advice versus rules

Technique and positioning articles are practical explainers, not personalised coaching. We avoid presenting one tactical preference as an official law of the sport.

Corrections

If a rule, product feature or factual statement is wrong or outdated, we want to correct it. Send corrections to support@padelwrist.com or use the public feedback board.

Use of AI

Software can help with research organisation, QA and structure, but published pages are expected to earn their place through useful synthesis, clear sourcing and editorial judgement. We do not publish bulk auto-generated pages simply because a keyword exists.
