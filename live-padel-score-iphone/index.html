Live Activities · PadelWrist 1.4

Keep the live padel score visible on iPhone.

PadelWrist can keep your active match close at hand through Live Activities and Dynamic Island on supported iPhones, while Apple Watch remains the quickest place to record each point.

Why a Live Activity helps during padel

You should not have to keep reopening an app just to check the match state. A Live Activity gives the current match a persistent, glanceable place on iPhone so the score remains easier to follow between rallies or from courtside.

Score on Apple Watch, follow on iPhone

PadelWrist is designed around different roles for different devices. Apple Watch is built for the scoring interaction itself: one quick update after the rally. iPhone can then keep the active match visible without becoming the thing you have to hold throughout play.

See how Apple Watch scoring works →

Dynamic Island on supported iPhones

On iPhones that support Dynamic Island, the current match can remain visible in that system surface as part of the Live Activity experience. Availability and presentation depend on the iPhone model and iOS behaviour.

Part of one match across your Apple devices

The Live Activity is not a separate scoreboard you need to maintain. It is another view of the same active PadelWrist match, alongside the Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad experiences.

After the match, switch from live score to Insights

Once the match ends, the useful question changes from “what is the score?” to “what does this result tell me?”. Match History and the dedicated Insights tab take over with recent form, player analysis, trends and post-match milestones.
