## The score should never become the distraction.

The point of a scorer is not to give you another screen to manage. It is to keep the match state obvious, make mistakes easy to correct, and get out of the way again.

**Play the rally. Tap the score. Move on.** Apple Watch keeps the live point score, server and important match states within one quick interaction. **Score from your wrist.** Start on one supported device, continue on another, and keep scoring locally through temporary connection loss. **One logical match, not three separate copies.**

## PadelWrist, explained simply.

The essentials about scoring, devices and what PadelWrist is designed to do.

What is PadelWrist?

PadelWrist is a padel scoring and match-tracking app for Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad. Score from your wrist, keep the live match visible on iPhone, then use Match History and Insights to understand your results afterwards.

Can I keep score in padel on Apple Watch?

Yes. Apple Watch is designed for quick in-match scoring, with the point score, server, important match states and spoken score announcements close at hand.

Does PadelWrist work if my devices temporarily disconnect?

Yes. Active scoring is local-first, so temporary connection loss does not stop the scoring device. Supported devices reconcile the current match again when connectivity returns.

Do I need a PadelWrist account?

No. You can score matches, use Match History and access the core PadelWrist experience without creating a PadelWrist account. Match history and saved players can sync privately through iCloud.

What is Fixed Points scoring?

Fixed Points counts every rally as one point instead of using love, 15, 30 and 40. It is useful for point-based social match formats.

Can I open PadelWrist from my Apple Watch face?

Yes. PadelWrist 1.7 includes circular, rectangular and inline complications, as well as Smart Stack access to jump into an active or new match.

What does Match Assistant do?

New in 1.8, Match Assistant guides you to the first server, the next server and changes of ends while the Apple Watch app is active. It can give a gentle haptic cue, and it has its own on/off setting.

Can I repeat a previous match?

Yes. Play Again is available in Match History on iPhone and iPad and directly on Apple Watch after completing a supported match.

Can I score in Spanish?

Yes. English and Spanish are supported across iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, including spoken score announcements and Live Activities.

Can friends follow my match live?

Yes. Share a six-digit Match Code so other PadelWrist users can join as players or spectators. The host decides who can score.

Is PadelWrist just for individual players?

PadelWrist 2.3 supports both individual match scoring and Americano and Mexicano Events for organisers. [Explore Events](https://padelwrist.com/events/) .

Can PadelWrist record a padel workout?

Yes. When scoring on Apple Watch, you can optionally record a workout in Apple Health. Workout recording is not required to keep score.
