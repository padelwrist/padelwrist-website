Apple Watch apps · Updated 20 August 2026

The best padel scorer is the one you barely notice.

Apple Watch padel apps now range from tiny Watch-only counters to full ecosystems with health tracking, live web scoreboards and tournament management. “Best” depends on which of those jobs you actually want.

Competitor feature claims were checked against current App Store listings on 18 August 2026. PadelWrist availability was updated on 20 August 2026.

A note on bias

PadelWrist publishes this comparison and makes one of the products. We will not pretend otherwise. Competitor descriptions below use their current public App Store claims, and we include limitations in our own product too.

Quick comparison

Padel - Score: Watch-only, minimalist scoring, automatic games/sets/tie-breaks, server/receiver indicators, side-change reminders and beta workout history.

Padel Score Watch Counter: iPhone, iPad and Watch, standalone Watch scoring, Star Point and multiple formats, live browser scoreboards, voice announcements, Health metrics, statistics and scheduling.

PadelScore: Score & Health: Watch-first scoring plus iPhone history, Health tracking, statistics, friends/leaderboards and Pro Americano/Mexicano tournament tools.

ScorePadel: Watch-only, paid once, no account or phone app, with standard scoring, deuce/advantage, tie-breaks and undo.

PadelWrist: Watch-first scoring with iPhone/iPad setup and continuity, Standard and Fixed Points, server tracking, spoken scores and local match history, with no account required. It does not currently manage tournaments or record Health workouts.

Padel - Score: minimal Watch-first scoring

Its current listing emphasises large readable scores, fast point entry, official scoring logic, server and receiver indicators, side-change reminders and match activity history in beta. It is a strong fit if you want the entire job to stay on the Watch with very little surrounding product.

Current App Store listing

Padel Score Watch Counter: broadest visible feature set

The current listing covers iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and browser scoreboards. It advertises standalone Watch scoring, Golden Point, Star Point, Americano, custom sets, health data, action-button scoring, voice announcements, detailed statistics, scheduling and sharing.

That breadth is genuinely useful if you want one product to cover scoring, fitness, analysis and courtside display. It also means it solves a substantially larger problem than a deliberately narrow scorer.

PadelScore: scoring, fitness and social tournaments

PadelScore combines Watch scoring with Health workout recording and an iPhone companion. Its August 2026 listing also advertises Americano and Mexicano tournament mode, pairings, rest rounds, standings, friends, a leaderboard, momentum data and detailed statistics through its Pro tier.

That makes it particularly relevant to somebody whose idea of “score app” includes fitness and organised social play.

ScorePadel: paid once, Watch only

ScorePadel takes almost the opposite approach: no subscription, no account and no phone companion. It focuses on Watch scoring, standard padel point logic, advantage sets, deuce counting and undo.

PadelWrist: match continuity across Apple devices

PadelWrist is built around a specific workflow: prepare on the larger screen when that helps, keep the live score on Apple Watch, and preserve the same logical match across supported Apple devices. It includes Standard and Fixed Points scoring, spoken scores, server tracking, correction and local history.

The scope is intentionally narrower than some competitors. There is currently no tournament organiser, social leaderboard, account system or Health workout tracking. The current release is focused on making scoring and match continuity dependable.

What should you compare?

Interaction: Can you add or correct a point quickly with wet or sweaty hands?

Visibility: Is score and server state readable without studying the Watch?

Offline behaviour: What happens when Watch and phone temporarily lose connection?

Format support: Standard, Golden Point, Star Point, Fixed Points, Americano or tournament management are not interchangeable.

After the match: Decide whether you want basic history, rich statistics, Health data, sharing or social rankings.

Business model: Free, paid once and subscription apps all exist in this category.

Our shortlist by use case

Pure Watch simplicity: Padel - Score and ScorePadel.

Broad all-in-one functionality: Padel Score Watch Counter.

Fitness plus Americano/Mexicano management: PadelScore.

Watch-first scoring plus iPhone/iPad continuity without an account: PadelWrist is designed specifically for that combination.
