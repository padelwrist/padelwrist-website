Apple Watch app guide

The best padel scoring app is the one you barely notice during the match.

There are now several credible Apple Watch padel scorers. They do not all solve the same problem, so “best” depends on whether you want pure wrist scoring, fitness data, tournament tools, multi-screen scoreboards or a companion app afterwards.

A note on bias

PadelWrist publishes this guide and, yes, we are building one of the apps in the category. Pretending this is an independent review would be silly. Instead, the comparison below focuses on publicly listed features and the different type of player each approach appears to suit. App features can change, so check the current App Store listing before choosing.

PadelWrist: multi-device scoring without an account

PadelWrist is built around Apple Watch as the primary live scoring surface, with iPhone and iPad used for setup, live companion scoring, saved players and match history. It supports Standard Match and Fixed Points, spoken scores, server tracking, undo and local-first scoring during temporary disconnection.

Its deliberate limitation is scope: it is a scorer and match companion, not currently a tournament organiser, social network or Health workout tracker. PadelWrist is coming soon to the App Store.

Padel - Score: Watch-only simplicity

Padel - Score is listed as an Apple Watch-only app. Its App Store description emphasises quick point entry, automatic games and sets, tie-breaks, server and receiver indicators, side-change reminders and a deliberately minimal Watch interface. It also describes workout and heart-rate recording as a beta feature.

It makes sense for somebody who wants the scoring job to live entirely on the Watch and does not need a phone companion.

Padel Score Watch Counter: the broad feature set

Padel Score Watch Counter spans iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch and advertises a large set of features: multiple scoring formats, live scoreboards, voice announcements, statistics, Health data, scheduling and sharing. It also supports web-browser scoreboards.

That breadth is attractive if you want one app to cover scoring, fitness, analysis and courtside display. Players who prefer a more deliberately narrow scorer may value simplicity over the extra layers.

PadelScore: Score & Health: scoring plus fitness and tournaments

PadelScore combines Apple Watch scoring with iPhone review, Health workout data, statistics and social features. Its current listing also describes Americano and Mexicano tournament functionality, friends and leaderboards.

It is particularly relevant if workout data and organised social formats matter as much as the live score.

ScorePadel: a focused paid Watch scorer

ScorePadel is another Watch-only approach. Its listing focuses on automatic points, games, sets, deuce, advantage, tie-breaks and undo, with no phone app or account. That makes it an interesting option for players who actively want to avoid a companion ecosystem.

What should you actually compare?

Start with the match itself. Can you score a point with one obvious action? Is the score readable at a glance? Can you undo a mistake without hunting through menus? Does the app clearly show the server? If the Watch disconnects from the phone, does scoring continue?

Then consider what happens before and after: player setup, history, statistics, Health integration, sharing, tournament management and whether those features are useful to you or simply more interface to navigate.

Our shortlist by use case

  • Want Watch-only simplicity: Padel - Score or ScorePadel are worth a look.
  • Want a very broad all-in-one feature set: Padel Score Watch Counter takes that approach.
  • Want fitness and tournament features: PadelScore: Score & Health explicitly targets both.
  • Want Watch-first scoring with iPhone/iPad match continuity and no account: that is the problem PadelWrist is designed around.

Choose the interaction, not the longest feature list.
