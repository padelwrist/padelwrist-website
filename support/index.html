Support

Let’s get you
back in the game.

Need help scoring a match, sharing results or running an Americano or Mexicano event on iPhone or iPad? Send us a note and we’ll do our best to help.

support@padelwrist.com ↗

We aim to reply within two working days.

Reporting an issue

Including a few details helps us understand what happened and respond more quickly:

  • 1
    What you were doingWhether you were scoring a match or running an event, its format, round and the step where the issue appeared.
  • 2
    What you expectedTell us what you thought should happen and what happened instead.
  • 3
    Your device detailsYour iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch model and software version, if known.
  • 4
    A screenshotIf possible, attach an image that shows the issue. Please remove personal information first.

Before you send

Please don’t include passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information in your message.

Help with PadelWrist Events

For Americano or Mexicano tournament issues, tell us the event format, number of players and courts, which round was affected and whether the issue involved a draw, a score, standings or read-only sharing. Include the app version and whether your device temporarily lost connectivity. You can omit real player names and should avoid sharing an active event code publicly.

Version 2.0 has been submitted for App Store review; Events will be available in the public app following approval and release. Explore the Events features or read our organiser checklist.
