Privacy

Privacy by design.

This policy explains how PADELWRIST handles information when you use the app, visit this website or contact us.

Last updated 12 August 2026
Contents Who we are On-device information App analytics Website analytics Support Lawful bases Sharing Retention Your rights Changes Contact
01

Who we are

PADELWRIST is operated in the United Kingdom and is the controller of personal information processed in connection with the PADELWRIST service.

PADELWRIST is designed to collect as little information as reasonably possible. You do not need to create an account to use the app, and we do not sell your personal information.

02

Information stored on your device

PADELWRIST stores information needed to provide its core functionality locally on your device. This may include player names you enter, match configuration, scores and results, match history, saved players, app preferences and settings.

We do not operate a PADELWRIST server that receives your player names, scores or match history. Where supported, match information may be transferred between your own iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch so that your devices can participate in the same match.

03

Product analytics in the app

PADELWRIST uses PostHog to help us understand how the app is used and improve the product. We send limited product-usage information such as app lifecycle activity, match format, whether a match is started or completed, feature usage, the type of Apple device used, general match duration, aggregate action counts and selected configuration options.

We deliberately minimise what is sent. PADELWRIST does not intentionally send PostHog player names, team names, individual scores, match or session identifiers, email addresses, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, HealthKit information or free-text match content.

PADELWRIST does not create PostHog user profiles or identify you by name or account. Session replay, screen capture and automatic interface-interaction recording are disabled. We do not use app analytics for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. PostHog is currently configured to use its European cloud infrastructure.

04

Error and crash reporting

On iPhone and iPad, PADELWRIST uses PostHog error tracking to help identify crashes and technical problems. Diagnostic information may include technical information associated with a crash or software error that is reasonably necessary to investigate and improve reliability.

We do not intentionally include player names, scores or other match content in crash reporting.

05

Website analytics and cookies

The PADELWRIST website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to help us understand how the website is used. Google Analytics may process pages viewed, visits and session activity, approximate geographic information, browser and device information, referring source, website interactions and a pseudonymous browser identifier stored using first-party cookies.

Google Analytics is optional and remains disabled unless you choose “Accept analytics” in the website's cookie controls. Rejecting analytics does not affect your ability to use the website.

If you accept, we use this information to understand website traffic and improve the site. We do not intentionally send names or email addresses to Google Analytics and advertising-related Google signals are disabled.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings control in the website footer. If you withdraw consent, Google Analytics is disabled and we attempt to remove analytics cookies set for this site.

06

Website hosting and technical information

When you visit the PADELWRIST website, our hosting provider may process standard technical information required to deliver and secure the website, including IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, date and time of the request, and server or security logs.

This information is used to operate, protect and deliver the website.

07

Support and feedback

If you contact us by email or send feedback, we may receive your email address, your name if you provide it, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to include.

We use this information to respond to your request, investigate reported issues, provide support and improve PADELWRIST. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for us to deal with your request.

08

Purchases and subscriptions

PADELWRIST does not currently collect your payment-card or bank-account details. If paid features or subscriptions are introduced through Apple's App Store, payment processing will be handled by Apple and PADELWRIST will not receive your full payment-card details.

If paid services materially change the information we process, this policy will be updated accordingly.

09

Information we do not currently collect through the app

PADELWRIST does not currently collect or use through the app HealthKit or health information, fitness or motion information, precise or approximate location, your phone contacts or address book, photos or videos, voice recordings, advertising identifiers, information for third-party advertising, or sensitive personal information.

If future features require additional types of information, this policy will be updated to reflect those features.

10

Our lawful bases for processing

Where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on appropriate lawful bases for processing personal information.

Providing the service and responding to you: where processing is necessary to provide something you have requested, such as responding to a support enquiry, we process the information necessary to deal with that request.

App analytics, diagnostics and improvement: where information processed through app analytics or diagnostics constitutes personal information, we rely on our legitimate interests in understanding how PADELWRIST is used, maintaining reliability, identifying and fixing technical problems and improving the product. We minimise the privacy impact by limiting the information collected and excluding unnecessary personal and match information.

Website analytics: where consent is required for Google Analytics cookies or similar technologies, we rely on your consent. You can withdraw that consent through the website's cookie controls.

Operating and securing our services: we rely on our legitimate interests in operating, maintaining and securing the PADELWRIST app and website. Where necessary, we may also process information to comply with a legal obligation.

11

Sharing information with third parties

We do not sell personal information.

We use third-party service providers where necessary to operate and improve PADELWRIST. These currently include PostHog for app product analytics, feature management and error tracking; Google Analytics for optional website analytics; and website hosting and email service providers for technical hosting and communications.

Service providers process information subject to their applicable contractual and data-protection obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law or valid legal process.

12

International data transfers

Some service providers we use, or their subprocessors, may process information outside the United Kingdom or European Economic Area. Where international transfers require safeguards, we use or rely on appropriate legal mechanisms such as UK or EU adequacy arrangements, approved contractual safeguards, or another mechanism permitted under applicable data-protection law.

13

How long we keep information

Information stored in PADELWRIST: match information and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you delete them using available app controls, remove the app, or the application data is otherwise erased.

Analytics and diagnostics: app analytics and diagnostic information is retained in accordance with our PostHog configuration and data-processing arrangements. Website analytics is retained in accordance with our Google Analytics configuration. We aim not to retain information for longer than reasonably necessary for analytics, troubleshooting and product improvement.

Support correspondence: support and feedback correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, investigate the issue, maintain appropriate records or meet legal requirements.

14

Your rights

Depending on where you live and the circumstances, you may have rights relating to personal information we hold about you. These may include rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict certain processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, receive certain information in a portable format where applicable, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and complain to a data-protection regulator.

These rights are not absolute and may depend on the circumstances and lawful basis being used. Because most PADELWRIST match information exists only on your own device and app analytics are intentionally designed not to identify individual users, we may not be able to identify particular analytics records as belonging to you.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). If you are in the European Economic Area, you may have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority in your country or region.

15

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed in connection with PADELWRIST. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information stored locally on your Apple devices is also subject to the security controls provided by your device and operating system.

16

Automated decision-making

PADELWRIST does not use personal information to make solely automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

17

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PADELWRIST or the services we use change. This could include introducing cloud synchronisation, health or fitness functionality, user accounts, subscriptions, advertising, additional analytics or diagnostics, or new third-party services.

The latest version will be published here with an updated “Last updated” date.

18

Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact support@padelwrist.com.
