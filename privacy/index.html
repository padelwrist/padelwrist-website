Overview

PadelWrist is a scoring app for Apple Watch and iPhone. This policy will explain what information the app processes, why it is processed, where it is stored and the choices available to you.

To refine: Add the legal name and location of the app operator, the policy’s effective date, and any territory-specific disclosures.

02

Information we process

Depending on the features included in the released app, PadelWrist may process information such as match scores, match formats, dates, durations and preferences you choose to enter.

The app may also receive limited technical information needed to operate, diagnose problems or respond to support requests.

To refine: Confirm whether match data stays only on-device, syncs through iCloud, or is sent to any PadelWrist service. List any diagnostics or analytics tools actually used.

03

How information is used

Information may be used to provide scoring and match-history features, remember your settings, keep devices in sync, improve reliability and respond when you ask for support.

PadelWrist will not use personal information for purposes that are materially different from those described in the final policy without providing appropriate notice.

04

Sharing and third parties

The final policy will identify every service provider or Apple service that can receive app data, explain why it is shared, and link to relevant third-party policies where appropriate.

05

Storage, security and retention

Reasonable safeguards will be used to protect information processed by PadelWrist. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

To refine: State where each data category is stored, how long it is retained, how deletion works, and what happens when the app is removed.

06

Your choices and rights

You may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or object to certain processing, depending on where you live. The final policy will describe how to exercise applicable rights and any in-app controls.

07

Children’s privacy

The final policy must state the intended age range for PadelWrist and describe how information relating to children is handled, in line with the app’s distribution settings and applicable law.

08

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as PadelWrist changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required.
