Privacy

Privacy by design.

This policy explains how PadelWrist handles information when you use the app, use optional features, visit this website or contact us.

Last updated 1 September 2026

01

Who we are

PadelWrist is operated in the United Kingdom and is the controller of personal information processed by us in connection with the PadelWrist service.

PadelWrist is designed to collect as little information as reasonably possible. You do not need to create an account to use the core app, and we do not sell your personal information.

02

App, player and match information

PadelWrist stores information needed to provide its scoring and match features. This may include player and team names you enter, match format and configuration, serving information, points, games and sets, scores and results, match duration and timestamps, match history, saved players, app preferences and settings.

Core match information is stored on your Apple devices and, when iCloud sync is enabled and available, match history and saved players may also be synchronised privately through Apple iCloud. PadelWrist does not require a PadelWrist account to use these features.

You control the information you enter. Player names do not need to be real names. If you enter information about another person, you should only enter information that is appropriate for use in the app.

03

Optional health, fitness and workout features

PadelWrist can record padel matches as workouts using Apple Health. These features are optional and may use Apple's HealthKit and related Apple fitness or workout frameworks. PadelWrist will request access only when it is needed for a feature you choose to use, and Apple will present the relevant system permission controls.

Depending on the feature and permissions you grant, PadelWrist may read or write health and fitness information needed to record or present your padel activity, such as workout activity, workout duration, active energy, heart-rate information or other workout metrics supported by the feature. The exact HealthKit data types requested are controlled through Apple's permission interface and may vary by device, operating-system version and feature.

HealthKit information is used only to provide health and fitness functionality. We do not use HealthKit information for advertising, marketing, profiling or cross-app tracking. We do not sell HealthKit information. We do not intentionally send HealthKit information to PostHog, Google Analytics or other product-analytics services.

HealthKit information remains subject to Apple's HealthKit privacy and permission controls. You can review or change PadelWrist's Health access through the Health app or your device's privacy settings. PadelWrist does not store personal HealthKit information in iCloud or use it for advertising or data-mining purposes.

04

Communication and synchronisation between your devices

PadelWrist may transfer match state and related app information between your own iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch so supported devices can participate in or display the same match. Apple frameworks may be used to provide this communication.

PadelWrist supports Apple-provided iCloud synchronisation for non-health app data such as match history and saved players. When you use this feature, that information may be stored and synchronised through Apple's iCloud services in accordance with your Apple account and iCloud settings. HealthKit-derived personal health information will not be included in PadelWrist cloud synchronisation.

If a future PadelWrist account or non-Apple cloud service materially changes how personal information is stored or transferred, we will update this policy and provide any permissions or notices required before using that service.

05

Product analytics in the app

PadelWrist uses PostHog to help us understand how the app is used, diagnose problems and improve the product. We may send limited product-usage information such as app lifecycle activity, app version, match format, whether a match is started or completed, feature usage, device or operating-system type, general match duration, aggregate action counts and selected non-sensitive configuration options.

We deliberately minimise what is sent. PadelWrist does not intentionally send PostHog player or team names, individual scores, match history content, email addresses, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, HealthKit information, heart-rate information or free-text match content.

PadelWrist does not use app analytics for third-party advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Session replay and screen recording are disabled. PostHog is currently configured to use its European cloud infrastructure.

Analytics systems may use a pseudonymous or randomly generated identifier, together with technical information, to distinguish app installations or sessions and understand aggregate product usage. We do not use that identifier to determine your real-world identity.

06

Error and crash reporting

PadelWrist uses diagnostic and error-reporting information to identify crashes, software errors and reliability problems. This may include app and operating-system versions, device type, technical error details, stack traces and limited technical context reasonably necessary to investigate a problem.

We do not intentionally include player names, individual scores, HealthKit information or other sensitive match content in crash reporting. Apple may also make aggregated or diagnostic information available to developers in accordance with the analytics choices you make on your Apple device.

07

Purchases, subscriptions and entitlements

Where PadelWrist offers paid features or subscriptions, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. PadelWrist does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

PadelWrist may receive or process information necessary to determine whether you are entitled to paid functionality, such as product or subscription status, purchase or transaction identifiers, renewal or expiry state and related App Store entitlement information. This is used to provide purchased features, restore purchases, manage subscription access, prevent fraud and provide support.

Your purchase relationship with Apple is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. You can manage App Store subscriptions using your Apple account settings.

08

Website analytics and cookies

The PadelWrist website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to help us understand how the website is used. Google Analytics may process pages viewed, visits and session activity, approximate geographic information, browser and device information, referring source, website interactions and a pseudonymous browser identifier stored using first-party cookies.

Google Analytics is optional and remains disabled unless you choose “Accept analytics” in the website's cookie controls. Rejecting analytics does not affect your ability to use the website.

If you accept, we use this information to understand website traffic and improve the site. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, match information or HealthKit information to Google Analytics, and advertising-related Google signals are disabled.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings control in the website footer. If you withdraw consent, Google Analytics is disabled and we attempt to remove analytics cookies set for this site.

09

Website hosting and technical information

When you visit the PadelWrist website, our hosting and security providers may process standard technical information required to deliver and protect the website, including IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, date and time of requests, and server or security logs.

This information is used to operate, secure and deliver the website, prevent abuse and maintain reliability.

10

Support and feedback

If you contact us by email or send feedback, we may receive your email address, your name if you provide it, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to include.

We use this information to respond to your request, investigate reported issues, provide support and improve PadelWrist. Please avoid sending health information or other sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for us to deal with your request.

11

Device permissions

Some PadelWrist features may require access to Apple system capabilities. Where permission is required, PadelWrist will request it through Apple's permission controls and explain why it is needed. Depending on the features available, this may include Health or fitness permissions and communication between your Apple devices.

You can change supported permissions through your Apple device settings. Refusing an optional permission may prevent the associated feature from working but should not prevent you from using unrelated PadelWrist features.

12

Advertising, tracking and other data

PadelWrist does not use your information for third-party advertising and does not sell personal information. We do not use advertising identifiers to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Unless a future feature clearly asks for it and this policy is updated where necessary, PadelWrist does not require access to your phone contacts or address book, photos or videos, microphone recordings or precise location for its core scoring functionality.

13

Our lawful bases for processing

Where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on appropriate lawful bases for processing personal information.

Providing the service and purchases: where processing is necessary to provide app functionality, paid features, purchase restoration or something else you have requested, we process the information necessary to provide that service or perform our contract with you.

Health and fitness information: optional HealthKit access is controlled by the permissions you grant through Apple. Where applicable data-protection law requires an additional lawful basis or condition for processing health information, we process it only for the health or fitness feature you have chosen and on the basis permitted by applicable law, including explicit consent where required.

App analytics, diagnostics and improvement: where information processed through app analytics or diagnostics constitutes personal information, we rely on our legitimate interests in understanding how PadelWrist is used, maintaining reliability, identifying and fixing technical problems and improving the product. We minimise the privacy impact by limiting the information collected and excluding unnecessary personal, health and match information.

Website analytics: where consent is required for Google Analytics cookies or similar technologies, we rely on your consent. You can withdraw that consent through the website's cookie controls.

Operating and securing our services: we rely on our legitimate interests in operating, maintaining, preventing abuse and securing PadelWrist. Where necessary, we may also process information to comply with a legal obligation.

14

Sharing information with third parties

We do not sell personal information.

We use third-party service providers where necessary to operate and improve PadelWrist. These may include PostHog for app product analytics and error tracking; Google Analytics for optional website analytics; Apple for App Store purchases, subscriptions and Apple-platform functionality; and website hosting, security and email service providers for technical hosting and communications.

We require service providers handling personal information on our behalf to provide appropriate protection consistent with applicable data-protection requirements. We may also disclose information where required by law or valid legal process.

HealthKit information is treated separately: we do not disclose information obtained through HealthKit to third parties for advertising, marketing or data brokerage. We will only share HealthKit-derived information where permitted by Apple, applicable law and the permissions or choices you have made.

15

International data transfers

Some service providers we use, or their subprocessors, may process information outside the United Kingdom or European Economic Area. Where international transfers require safeguards, we use or rely on appropriate legal mechanisms such as UK or EU adequacy arrangements, approved contractual safeguards, or another mechanism permitted under applicable data-protection law.

16

How long we keep information

Local app information: match information, saved players and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you delete them using available app controls, remove the app, or the application data is otherwise erased. Information synchronised between your devices may remain on those devices until separately removed or erased.

Health and fitness information: HealthKit information is managed through Apple's HealthKit store and your device permissions. PadelWrist does not intentionally retain HealthKit information in our product-analytics systems or store personal HealthKit information in iCloud.

Purchases: entitlement and transaction information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide or restore paid functionality, maintain records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

Analytics and diagnostics: app analytics and diagnostic information is retained in accordance with our PostHog configuration and data-processing arrangements. Website analytics is retained in accordance with our Google Analytics configuration. We aim not to retain information for longer than reasonably necessary for analytics, troubleshooting, security and product improvement.

Support correspondence: support and feedback correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, investigate the issue, maintain appropriate records or meet legal requirements.

17

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live and the circumstances, you may have rights relating to personal information we hold about you. These may include rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict certain processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, receive certain information in a portable format where applicable, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and complain to a data-protection regulator.

These rights are not absolute and may depend on the circumstances and lawful basis being used. Because much of your PadelWrist information exists only on your own devices and app analytics are deliberately designed to minimise identification, we may not hold information that enables us to identify a particular analytics record as yours.

You can manage local app information using available app controls, manage HealthKit access through Apple's Health and privacy settings, manage App Store subscriptions through Apple, and withdraw website analytics consent using the website's Cookie settings control.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). If you are in the European Economic Area, you may have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority in your country or region.

18

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed in connection with PadelWrist. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information stored locally on your Apple devices is also subject to the security controls provided by your device and operating system.

19

Automated decision-making

PadelWrist does not use personal information to make solely automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

20

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PadelWrist develops, including when features, service providers or legal requirements change. Some sections describe optional functionality that may not yet be available on every device or app version so that you can understand how those features will handle information when they become available.

Where a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will provide additional notice or request permission where required. The latest version will be published here with an updated “Last updated” date.

21

Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact support@padelwrist.com.
