# Keep score from your wrist.

Score each point quickly on Apple Watch. PadelWrist 1.8 adds Match Assistant to help with serving order and changes of ends, while iPhone keeps the match visible and Insights help you learn afterwards.

## Why score padel on Apple Watch?

Padel moves quickly, and stopping to unlock a phone breaks the rhythm of the match. A wrist-first score app keeps the controls close without making the device the centre of attention.

With PadelWrist, Apple Watch is designed for the actions you need during play: add a point, correct a mistake, check the score and keep track of who is serving.

## Score, server and spoken score in one place

PadelWrist supports standard padel games, sets and tie-breaks. The app tracks the serving side and can announce the score aloud, helping all four players stay aligned without repeatedly checking a screen.

If a point is entered incorrectly, undo and correction controls let you fix it quickly and continue.

## Meet Match Assistant in version 1.8

Match Assistant helps you keep track of who serves and when to switch ends during a padel match. When play begins, the Watch can show who should serve first. As points, games and tie-breaks progress, it can prompt the next server or remind you to change ends.

When a service change and an ends change happen together, PadelWrist combines them into one clear instruction. The prompt appears briefly beneath the score, without covering the scoring buttons, serving information or Undo control.

One subtle haptic can draw your attention to new guidance while the Watch app is active. Match Assistant is on by default, with a separate on/off toggle on Apple Watch. Turning it off does not change spoken score announcements, which remain a separate preference.

Prompts are available in English and Spanish, with accessible full player and team names. They are deliberately brief and do not replay after a correction, Undo, reopening History or reconnecting your devices. If a prompt is missed, the score and serving information still update normally.

** During live play only: ** Match Assistant is a foreground feature and is best effort. It does not send background notifications or guarantee haptics when the Watch app is inactive. It does not add spoken Match Assistant instructions.

## More reliable serving order

Version 1.8 also improves how the app preserves individual serving rotation when you edit a result in Match History or move from a tie-break into the next set. The established serving order remains consistent with the match progress and any manual player-order corrections.

## Open PadelWrist from your Watch face

Version 1.7 adds circular, rectangular and inline complications that put PadelWrist on your Apple Watch face. You can also launch the app from Smart Stack.

These shortcuts make it quicker to return to an active match or set up a new one. They are available in English and Spanish, alongside the rest of PadelWrist’s scoring experience.

## Start on iPhone. Continue on Apple Watch.

You do not have to configure every match on the smallest screen. Set up players and the match on iPhone or iPad, prepare it for Apple Watch, then start scoring from your wrist.

You can also begin on Apple Watch and later open the active match on iPhone. PadelWrist is built around one logical match rather than separate Watch and phone versions.

## Temporary disconnection does not stop the match

Scoring is local-first. If your devices temporarily lose their connection, the scoring device can continue recording the match. When connectivity returns, supported devices reconcile the current state.

That makes PadelWrist useful on court even when the connection between devices is not perfect.

## From wrist-first scoring to a complete match record

Apple Watch is the primary live scoring surface rather than a dense statistics dashboard. The Watch still keeps the score, server and next scoring action as the priority. Match History, Live Activities and Insights add value around the live match without making the Watch interface heavier.

Optional Health and workout integration lets players connect a scored match with relevant workout context when they choose to. Health data is not required to use PadelWrist, and the core scoring experience remains independent of it.

During the match, supported iPhones can keep the score visible through Live Activities and Dynamic Island. After the match, iPhone and iPad provide the space for searchable history, recent form, player analysis and post-match Insights.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I keep score in padel on Apple Watch?

Yes. PadelWrist lets you score directly from Apple Watch using simple wrist-first controls.

### Does Match Assistant announce instructions out loud?

No. Match Assistant uses short onscreen prompts and optional haptics while the Watch app is active. Spoken score announcements are separate.

### Can I turn Match Assistant off?

Yes. Use the Watch Match Assistant toggle, independently of spoken scoring. It is on by default.

### Can I add PadelWrist to my Watch face?

Yes. In version 1.7 you can use Watch face complications or Smart Stack to open PadelWrist quickly.

### Do I need an account?

No. PadelWrist does not require an account to start scoring.

### Can I use iPhone and Apple Watch for the same match?

Yes. The active match can move between supported Apple devices, with iPhone and Apple Watch synchronising when connectivity is available.
