# Keep every point of the match clear.

PadelWrist is a padel score tracker for Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad. It handles the match state for you so you can focus on playing rather than remembering whether it is 30–40, deuce, game point or tie-break.

## What should a padel score tracker do?

Padel uses a familiar tennis-style scoring structure, but keeping track during a fast match can still be surprisingly easy to lose. A good score tracker should make the current point score obvious, handle games and sets automatically and keep the serving side clear.

PadelWrist is built around that job. Standard Match follows points, games, sets and tie-breaks, while Fixed Points provides a simpler running score for point-based social formats.

## Track the score without stopping the match

Apple Watch is the primary live scoring surface. Large scoring regions keep each point close to hand, while the current score, server and important match states stay visible without filling the screen with unnecessary controls.

If you enter a point incorrectly, Undo and correction controls help you fix it without rebuilding the match from scratch.

## Let Match Assistant handle the reminders

PadelWrist 1.8 adds Match Assistant for Apple Watch. While you are actively using the Watch app, it can show the first server, tell you who serves next and remind you when to change ends. Where both happen together, you get a single combined instruction.

A short haptic can draw attention to a new prompt without interrupting the rally. Match Assistant is optional and independent of spoken score announcements. It does not send background notifications.

The update also improves individual serving-order accuracy after edits and tie-breaks. [See how Match Assistant works.](https://padelwrist.com/apple-watch-padel-scoring/#match-assistant)

## Use the right Apple device for the moment

PadelWrist is not limited to the Watch. You can set up or score on iPhone and iPad, prepare a match on a larger screen for Apple Watch, or open an active Watch-started match on iPhone.

The aim is one logical match across the supported devices, rather than separate scores that you have to keep aligned manually.

## Keep a useful padel match history

Finishing the match does not make the score disappear. PadelWrist keeps local match history on iPhone and iPad so you can review set-by-set results and reopen previous matches later.

You can also resume unfinished matches, reuse saved players and correct historic results when something was entered incorrectly.

## Padel score tracker FAQs

### Can I use PadelWrist as a padel counter on Apple Watch?

Yes. Apple Watch is designed as the quickest way to add points during a live match while PadelWrist handles the scoring structure around them.

### Does it track who is serving?

Yes. Server state is part of the live match, and Match Assistant in version 1.8 can help with first and next servers and changes of ends while the Watch app is active.

### Does it work without an account?

Yes. Scoring and local match history do not require a PadelWrist account.

### Does it organise a full Americano or Mexicano tournament?

Not currently. Fixed Points can score an individual point-based match, but PadelWrist does not currently create pairings, rotate partners or manage tournament standings.
