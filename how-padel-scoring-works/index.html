Padel scoring explained

How padel scoring works.

Padel uses a familiar tennis-style scoring system, but it is easy to lose track once a rally starts. Here is the standard structure from a single point through to the full match.

Points: 15, 30, 40 and game

In standard padel, points progress as 15, 30, 40, game. A team needs to win enough points to take the game. If both teams reach 40, the game reaches deuce under traditional advantage scoring.

Different competitions may use alternative deciding-point rules, so the match format should always be agreed before play.

Games make up a set

A set is normally won by the first team to reach six games with at least a two-game lead. Scores such as 6-0, 6-1, 6-2, 6-3 and 6-4 therefore complete the set.

If the score reaches 5-5, a team generally needs to win 7-5, or the set moves to a tie-break at 6-6.

What happens at 6-6?

At 6-6, a tie-break is commonly played. Tie-break points use ordinary numbers rather than 15, 30 and 40. The usual aim is to reach seven points with a two-point advantage.

PADELWRIST handles the game, set and tie-break state for you so the scoreboard can move forward without players calculating the match state manually.

Best of three sets

A standard padel match is commonly played as best of three sets. The first team to win two sets wins the match, so a straight-sets result might finish 6-3, 6-4, while a three-set match could finish 6-4, 3-6, 6-2.

Who serves in padel?

Service alternates by game according to the agreed serving order. Keeping track of the server can become surprisingly easy to forget during a long match, which is why PADELWRIST includes server tracking alongside the score.

Standard scoring versus Fixed Points

PADELWRIST also supports Fixed Points scoring for point-based match formats such as Americano-style and Mexicano-style games. Fixed Points tracks a target number of points rather than pretending those formats use standard games and sets.

PADELWRIST does not currently organise a full Americano or Mexicano tournament, rotate partners or generate standings. It provides the scoring layer for an individual fixed-points match.

Frequently asked questions

How many games do you need to win a padel set?

Normally six games with a two-game lead. A set can therefore finish 6-4, but not 6-5. At 5-5 it may finish 7-5, while 6-6 usually leads to a tie-break.

How many sets do you need to win?

In a best-of-three match, two sets.
