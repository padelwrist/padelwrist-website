# PadelWrist product context

## Product

PadelWrist is a padel scoring app for Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad. It is designed to let players keep score with minimal distraction during a match, while using larger Apple devices for setup, history and management.

PadelWrist is live on the Apple App Store: https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/padelwrist/id6799725420

App Store ID: `6799725420`.

## Audience

Padel players who want a simple, premium scoring experience without repeatedly handling a phone during play. The product should feel immediately understandable to recreational players while remaining polished enough for frequent players.

## Primary promise

**Keep the score. Stay in the match.**

The website should make three ideas obvious:

1. Score quickly from Apple Watch.
2. Keep one logical active match moving across supported Apple devices.
3. Keep useful match history without requiring an account.

## Product truth

- Available on the Apple App Store.
- Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad support.
- Standard padel scoring with games, sets and tie-breaks.
- Fixed Points scoring for point-based formats.
- Server tracking and spoken score announcements.
- Undo and score correction.
- Match history, saved players, historic editing and unfinished-match resume.
- Local-first scoring during temporary device disconnection.
- No account required.

Do not imply tournament management, social networking, cloud accounts, automatic Americano/Mexicano partner rotation, Apple Health features or other capabilities that are not part of the released product.

## Brand naming

Use **PadelWrist** everywhere the product name appears in ordinary copy, metadata, accessibility labels and documentation.

Use **PADELWRIST** only when rendering the visual wordmark itself.

## Website mode

The homepage is a **Persuade** surface. Product imagery, hierarchy and confidence matter more than exhaustive feature documentation. Supporting guides are **Read** surfaces and should prioritise clarity and comfortable reading.
