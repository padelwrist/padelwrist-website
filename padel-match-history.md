# Your padel history should become more useful over time.

Every scored match is saved as something you can find, review and learn from later. Search and filter your results, add useful context and move naturally from one match into the bigger patterns in Insights.

## Search instead of scroll

Once you have more than a handful of matches, a chronological list is not enough. PadelWrist lets you search and filter Match History by date, format, result, players and other useful criteria so you can get back to the result you actually care about.

## Add the context the score cannot explain

A 6-4, 6-3 result does not tell you whether it was a league match, a casual game or your first time playing on the left. Add match type, your playing side and private notes so the result still makes sense months later.

## Review, correct and resume

You can revisit completed matches, correct saved set scores when something was entered incorrectly and resume unfinished matches rather than rebuilding them from the beginning.

## Play Again: reuse the setup, not the old score

New in PadelWrist 1.9, Play Again sits beneath the result in Match History on iPhone and iPad. It reopens the familiar match setup with available rules, player choices and team names ready for review. Make any changes you need, then start a fresh match from zero.

The original result stays saved. An active match is not silently replaced: PadelWrist asks you to confirm if starting or preparing the rematch would end the current session. On iPhone, you can also prepare the new setup for Apple Watch. [ See the Play Again guide ](https://padelwrist.com/play-again-padel-match/) .

## History feeds Insights

Your history is the foundation for recent form, performance trends, partner and opponent records, head-to-head analysis and post-match milestones. The more accurately you score and identify players, the more useful those views become.

[ Explore PadelWrist Insights → ](https://padelwrist.com/padel-player-statistics/)

## Sync privately with iCloud

Match history and saved players can sync privately through iCloud across supported Apple devices. You do not need to create a separate PadelWrist account just to keep your history available.

## Keep control of your records

You can remove individual matches and clear stored history when you no longer want it. Private notes are there for your own context, not as content for a public profile.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I filter by player?

Yes. PadelWrist includes Match History search and filters covering players alongside date, format, result and more.

### Can I add notes after a match?

Yes. Match details can include private notes, match type and your playing side.

### Can I start a rematch from History?

Yes. In version 1.9, open a previous result on iPhone or iPad and tap Play Again. Review the setup and start a separate match, or prepare for Watch from iPhone.

### Does Match History work with Insights?

Yes. Recorded matches provide the underlying data used for form, trends and player analysis.

## Score once. Use the result again.

Start on Apple Watch, then use iPhone or iPad to search the result, add context and understand the pattern behind it.
