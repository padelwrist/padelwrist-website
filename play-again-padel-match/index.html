PadelWrist 1.9 · Match History guide

Play Again: repeat a padel match without starting from scratch.

A rematch should take less setup than the original. Play Again brings back the match format, player choices and available rules from an earlier result, then lets you check everything before scoring a new game.

Where to find Play Again

On iPhone or iPad, open PadelWrist and go to Match History. Choose the previous match you want to repeat, then tap Play Again beneath the result. It works from completed matches, drawn matches and matches ended early, provided you can review a valid new setup.

You are not resuming the old score. PadelWrist opens the familiar match configuration screen so you can check the setup before creating an entirely separate match.

What gets carried into the new setup?

Play Again reuses available information about your Standard Match or Fixed Points configuration, including the format, supported scoring rules, saved-player selections and custom team names. You can keep those settings or adjust them. You can switch formats during review when the new match calls for a different structure.

Saved Players are linked to their identities rather than guessed from display names. If a player was renamed, their current name is shown. If someone was removed or is no longer available, PadelWrist asks you to choose an appropriate replacement rather than silently assigning somebody else.

Start the rematch or prepare it for your Watch

Once you have checked the lineup and rules, start the match. The new session begins at zero, with a separate match identity and its own History record when saved. The old result stays available for review and Insights. Any old points, sets, private notes or Undo actions are not transferred into the rematch.

On iPhone you can choose Prepare for Watch instead. That sends the reviewed configuration through the established preparation journey, with scoring beginning on Apple Watch. On iPad, Play Again supports local scoring and the existing court-side experience; there is no direct iPad-to-iPhone Watch preparation relay.

What happens if you already have a match running?

Opening Play Again does not end an existing match. If you try to start or prepare another while a match is active, PadelWrist asks you to confirm replacing the current session. Cancelling that confirmation preserves the active score. If you confirm, the current match is ended using the existing match lifecycle and its progress remains in History.

That makes the flow useful between social games without casually overwriting something in progress. The review stage itself is safe to leave without creating another match.

Playing again from older History records

Older matches may not contain every original setting. Rather than reconstructing a missing rule from the final score, PadelWrist uses saved details it can verify and shows a review notice where necessary. Standard Match defaults or a normal Fixed Points target can fill gaps, but you remain in control of the setup.

For example, a stored match may tell PadelWrist which players participated but not whether you originally selected a random first server. In that case the rematch review does not pretend to know the earlier choice. Check any missing settings before you start.

Play Again or Resume Match?

These actions solve different problems. Resume Match continues an unfinished match from its existing score and progress. Play Again starts a different match, from zero, based on a previous setup. Use Resume when play was interrupted; use Play Again for another game, a rematch or a repeated weekly pairing.

Direct Play Again on the Watch is separate development work and should not be confused with preparing a new rematch from iPhone for Watch scoring.

Make the next game easier.

Open Match History on iPhone or iPad, choose the result and select Play Again. You can keep building useful Insights as new results are saved.
