# How to Communicate With Your Padel Partner | PadelWrist

Doubles tactics · Updated 27 August 2026

# The best call is short, early and useful.

Your partner cannot always see both opponents while moving for a lob, bandeja or ball off the glass. Communication fills in the information they are missing and helps the pair move before the next shot happens.

## Call what your partner cannot see

If your partner is facing the back wall or tracking a lob, tell them whether the opponents are staying back, moving to the net or splitting. That information changes whether they should attack, reset or play safely.

## Use a small shared vocabulary

Simple calls work because there is no translation delay. “Mine”, “yours”, “back”, “stay”, “both up”, “one back” and “middle” are more useful than a full sentence during a fast rally.

The exact words do not matter as much as both players agreeing what they mean.

## Call middle balls before both players swing

The middle is valuable tactically but can create uncertainty inside your own pair. Agree who normally takes a high central ball, who owns lower defensive balls and how handedness changes that priority.

## Help on lobs

A player tracking a lob may lose sight of the opponents. Tell them if the opponents are charging forward, staying deep or if there is time for a controlled overhead. The information should arrive while they are moving, not after they have hit.

## Do not coach every shot during the rally

Constant instructions can become noise. Calls should describe useful information or resolve ownership. Technique advice belongs between points, not while your partner is trying to contact the ball.

## Reset quickly after mistakes

Partners will collide, leave balls and make bad calls. A brief “my fault” or clarification of the next ball is more useful than replaying the argument during the next point.

## Agree calls before the match

You do not need a tactical meeting. Spend half a minute agreeing how you call middle balls, whether you want opponent positions called on lobs and what words mean “stay” or “switch”. That small bit of preparation is especially useful with a new partner because habits differ between players.

## Use the score as shared information too

Say the score clearly before serving. Confirm unusual formats such as Golden Point, Star Point or Fixed Points before the match. Good communication is not only tactical; it also prevents avoidable score and rotation confusion.
