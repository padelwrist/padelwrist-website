Available now · PadelWrist 1.4

Every match tells you more.

Version 1.4 builds on PadelWrist's wrist-first scoring with a dedicated Insights experience, much smarter Match History and a better way to keep the current match visible on iPhone.

A dedicated Insights tab

Insights now has its own place in PadelWrist. See performance trends, recent form and player analysis without digging through individual match results.

Compare how your results change over time, explore different scoring formats and understand your records with regular partners and opponents.

Explore PadelWrist Insights →

Post-match insights that surface the interesting bits

A completed match can now highlight milestones, winning streaks and notable form. The aim is not to bury you in invented metrics, but to surface useful patterns from the score and match information PadelWrist actually records.

Search and filter Match History

As your history grows, finding one result matters more. Version 1.4 lets you search and filter by date, format, result, players and other useful criteria instead of scrolling through a flat list.

You can also add more context to a match, including match type, your playing side and private notes.

Explore Match History →

Live Activities and Dynamic Island

On supported iPhones, your current match can stay visible through Live Activities and Dynamic Island. Apple Watch remains the fastest place to score each point, while iPhone gives you a glanceable live view away from the app.

See how Live Activities fit into PadelWrist →

The 1.3 foundations are still here

Version 1.4 builds on the major features introduced in 1.3: live match sharing with a Match Code, Apple Health workout recording, private iCloud sync for match history and saved players, polished match result sharing and expanded scoring options including Advantage, Golden Point, Star Point and match tie-breaks.

Explore match sharing →

Still built around the match, not the app

PadelWrist remains deliberately focused. During play, the score should be quick to update and easy to trust. The deeper tools belong before and after the rally, where iPhone and iPad have space for history, Insights and context without crowding the Apple Watch experience.

Update to PadelWrist 1.4

The latest version is available now on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
