Available now · PadelWrist 1.3

PadelWrist 1.3 is here.

PadelWrist 1.3 is a more complete match companion: still fast and focused during play, but much more useful when you want to understand, share or review the match afterwards.

The complete 1.3 experience

The App Store release combines the core PadelWrist experience with: Standard Match and Fixed Points scoring, Apple Watch-first live controls, server tracking, spoken score announcements, undo and correction, saved players, local match history, resume and cross-device continuity between supported Apple devices.

Those fundamentals remain fast and dependable. Version 1.3 builds around them rather than making live scoring heavier.

Player Intelligence

The biggest step is richer analysis on iPhone and iPad. Player Intelligence includes performance summaries, trends over time, partner and opponent analysis, head-to-head records, point timelines, momentum and advanced filters.

The product will stay honest about what it can infer. PadelWrist records scoring events, not every shot, position or tactical decision, so statistics will be based on information the match data can genuinely support rather than pretending to know more than it does.

Read more about Player Intelligence →

Match sharing

Sharing is now a larger part of the post-match experience. Version 1.3 includes polished result cards and shareable match summaries, plus live match sharing flows where another person can follow a hosted match without the core product becoming an account-led social network.

Preview match sharing →

More scoring-rule control

Standard Match and Fixed Points remain the core modes, but players do not all use exactly the same club rules. Version 1.3 includes broader scoring-rule configuration so the setup can better match the game being played while the live screen stays simple.

This is intentionally different from adding a separate mode for every social format. Americano and Mexicano are still represented by generic Fixed Points unless a future release explicitly introduces tournament management.

Optional Health and workout context

Health-related functionality is available as an optional layer around the scored match. Players can connect useful workout context where it adds value, without making Health access a requirement for scoring and without crowding the Apple Watch live-match interface.

Apple Watch remains a sporting instrument first: score, server, match state and the next interaction stay visually dominant.

What remains free and focused

The product direction is to keep the core scoring experience strong rather than making basic match scoring artificially restrictive. Standard and Fixed Points scoring, the live scoreboard, serving, announcements, correction, saved players, history and basic sharing are the foundation of PadelWrist.

Version 1.3 also introduces deeper intelligence and analysis while keeping the core scoring experience strong.

Still deliberately not a booking or tournament platform

PadelWrist is focused on the match itself. It is not trying to become a court-booking marketplace, public social network or full tournament-management system. That focus keeps the live experience quick and leaves room for deeper analysis after the match without making setup or scoring heavier.

Help shape what comes next

PadelWrist is still evolving. Feedback on the current app, awkward moments during a match and the features you would genuinely use all help prioritise what comes next.
