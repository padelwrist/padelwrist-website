29 October 2026 · Americano and Mexicano Events

Version 2.0

Version 2.0 introduced native Events for clubs, coaches and tournament hosts on iPhone and iPad. Run Americano and Mexicano sessions with players, courts, round-by-round fixtures, official results and individual standings in one place.

  • Organise an event: Choose Americano or Mexicano, enter players, courts and your point target.
  • Review smart draws: Plan Americano rotations or let confirmed standings inform later Mexicano pairings.
  • Keep each round moving: Publish court assignments, record official scores and progress to the next draw when ready.
  • Share with participants: Players can follow published fixtures, courts, results and standings by code or link in read-only mode.
  • Handle real club sessions: Manage resting players, withdrawals, score corrections and early finishes.
  • Finish with the results: Review the final individual leaderboard, podium and completed event history.

Apple Watch match scoring, Match Assistant, live match sharing, History, Insights and Play Again remain available. Events is managed on iPhone and iPad, not on Apple Watch.

19 October 2026 · Play Again

Version 1.9

Ready for another game? Open a previous match in History on iPhone or iPad and choose Play Again. PadelWrist brings its match setup back for review, so you can get the next match underway without rebuilding everything from scratch.

  • Find Play Again directly beneath the result in Match History, including finished, drawn or ended-early matches
  • Reuse saved players, teams and available match settings for Standard Match or Fixed Points
  • Review and edit the format, players, rules or team names before starting
  • Start a separate new match from zero while preserving the original result in History
  • On iPhone, prepare the rematch for Apple Watch using the existing Watch preparation flow
  • Protect an existing active match with a confirmation before replacing it
  • Handle older matches and unavailable players with clear review prompts instead of guessing

Version 1.9 introduced Play Again in iPhone and iPad History; direct Apple Watch Play Again followed in 2.2.

18 October 2026 · Match Assistant

Version 1.8

Meet Match Assistant on Apple Watch. Get timely guidance about who serves and when to change ends, so you can concentrate on playing rather than working out the rotation.

  • First-server guidance when a new match begins
  • Next-server prompts as the serving order changes
  • Change-ends reminders, including one combined prompt when ends and server change together
  • One subtle attention haptic for each fresh prompt while the Watch app is active
  • A separate Match Assistant toggle on Apple Watch, independent of spoken scores
  • English and Spanish instructions, with accessible full player and team names
  • Improvements to individual service order after editing a match in History and at tie-break/set transitions
  • Guidance that clears automatically and does not replay after Undo, corrections, resuming matches or reconnecting devices

Match Assistant is a foreground, best-effort Watch feature. It does not send background notifications or guarantee haptics when the app is inactive. Live scores and server tracking continue even if a prompt is missed.
