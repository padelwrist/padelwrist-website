# Share the score. Stay in the match.

Give other PadelWrist users a way to follow the score as it happens, or share a finished result without creating a public player profile.

## One match, one six-digit code

Share a live match using a six-digit Match Code. Other PadelWrist users can join as players or spectators, so everyone follows the same match rather than comparing separate scoreboards.

The host controls who can score. Spectators can follow without taking over the match.

## Keep scoring from your Apple Watch

Apple Watch remains the quickest place to record each rally. Live sharing works alongside the match, so the score can be followed by others while you focus on playing.

On supported iPhones, Live Activities and Dynamic Island can also keep the current match visible at a glance.

## Send the result afterwards

When the match finishes, turn the score into a polished result card and share it with friends using the standard Apple sharing options. Match History preserves the result, and Insights help you see the bigger picture over time.

## Your match, without a social feed

PadelWrist does not require a public player profile for core scoring, history or result sharing. It is designed to make the useful details easy to share without turning your matches into a social network.

## Try live match sharing

Score from your wrist, invite other users with a Match Code and share the result once the match is over.
