Padel scoring rules

Padel tie-break rules, without the head-scratching.

A standard padel tie-break usually arrives when a set reaches 6-6. From there the familiar 15, 30, 40 disappears and every point is counted as a number.

How do you win a padel tie-break?

The first pair to reach seven points wins the tie-break only if they lead by at least two. So 7-5 ends it; 7-6 does not. At 6-6, play continues until one pair creates that two-point margin: 8-6, 9-7, 10-8 and so on.

Winning the standard tie-break gives the pair the set 7-6.

Who serves first?

The player whose turn it is to serve starts the tie-break and serves one point from the right-hand side. After that, service passes to the opposing pair for two points. Players then continue serving two points at a time, keeping the service order already established in the set.

This one-two-two-two pattern is the easiest way to remember it: one point for the opening server, then pairs of points thereafter.

When do you change ends?

Players change ends after every six points in the tie-break. That means after 6 total points, 12, 18 and so on. There is no normal change-of-ends rest during the tie-break.

What happens in the next set?

The pair that did not begin serving the tie-break starts serving in the following set. The players can choose their internal serving order again at the start of the new set, as normal.

What is a super tie-break?

A super tie-break is commonly used instead of a deciding third set when that format has been agreed in advance. Under the FIP alternative scoring methods, it is played to ten points, again requiring a two-point margin. A normal seven-point match tie-break is also an allowed alternative.

The important distinction is that a standard tie-break normally decides a set at 6-6, while a match tie-break or super tie-break can replace an entire deciding set.

Quick example

Imagine a set reaches 6-6. Team One wins the tie-break 7-4. The set is recorded as 7-6 to Team One. If the tie-break instead reaches 6-6, the next point makes it 7-6 but the match continues. Team One would still need another point to reach 8-6 and finish it.
