Common club questions

What to know before you plan.

Is PadelWrist Events available to clubs now?

No. PadelWrist Events is in development. Clubs and hosts can currently use the released app to score individual matches, but full tournament administration is not publicly available.

Which tournament formats are being explored?

Americano and Mexicano are the initial focus. Americano uses planned partnership rotations; Mexicano uses confirmed standings to propose later pairings, subject to organiser review.

Will players be able to see their courts and partners?

The proposed Events experience includes published round assignments and optional read-only participant information. These capabilities are being developed and are not yet released.

Can I share what my club needs?

Yes. Use the Events feedback email or the feature suggestion channel to describe how you organise groups, courts, scoring and standings. This is feedback, not event registration.
