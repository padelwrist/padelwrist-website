Common club questions

Plan your next social with confidence.

Can I organise a tournament with PadelWrist today?

PadelWrist 2.0, which introduces Events, has been submitted to Apple for review. The App Store version currently supports individual scoring; tournament administration becomes available when 2.0 is approved and released.

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

Americano typically follows a planned rotation of partners and opponents. Mexicano uses the confirmed standings to propose later pairings. In both formats, the organiser reviews the draw before publishing it.

Can players follow their courts and standings?

Version 2.0 includes read-only event following through a code or link, so participants can view published courts, partners, results and standings without changing official scores.

Do clubs need court booking or payment software?

No booking or event-fee collection is provided by Events. PadelWrist organises the sporting side of your social, not venue reservations or payments.
