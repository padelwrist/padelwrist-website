Available now · PadelWrist 1.3

Turn match history into useful Player Intelligence.

The final score tells you who won. Player Intelligence helps you understand the patterns around the matches you play without pretending PadelWrist records data it does not have.

From saved matches to patterns over time

PadelWrist already keeps local match history on iPhone and iPad. Version 1.3 builds on those recorded matches with richer summaries and filters so previous results become easier to compare rather than remaining a flat list.

Available views include performance trends over time, match and format patterns and summaries that make recurring results easier to understand at a glance.

Partner and opponent analysis

Saved players make it possible to ask more useful questions: how do results change with a particular partner, how often have you played the same opponents, and what does the head-to-head record look like?

PadelWrist surfaces those relationships locally from the matches PadelWrist actually knows about, rather than creating public player profiles or a social ranking system.

Head-to-head, timelines and momentum

Where the scoring history supports it, Player Intelligence can show head-to-head records and the flow of a match through a point timeline. Momentum views can then help visualise runs and changes in control without claiming to measure tactics, shot quality or player movement.

That distinction matters. PadelWrist knows scoring events and match state; it does not automatically know whether a point came from a smash, volley, forced error or positioning mistake.

Advanced filters without dashboard overload

Richer filters let players who want to explore by date, format, partner, opponent and other match attributes. These belong on iPhone and iPad, where the larger canvas can support analysis properly.

Apple Watch remains focused on the live match. Statistics should never compete with the score or make scoring slower during play.

Privacy remains part of the product

The current product does not require an account for core scoring and keeps match history local. Player Intelligence follows that same privacy-conscious approach. A richer statistics experience does not automatically require a public profile or social backend.

Available in version 1.3

You can save matches, review results, edit historic set scores, resume unfinished matches and use Player Intelligence to explore the patterns around those results in more depth.
