PadelWrist Insights · Version 1.4

Do more with every match you score.

Scoring a match should leave you with more than a final result. PadelWrist Insights turns your Match History into recent form, performance trends and useful player analysis without requiring manual spreadsheets or a public player profile.

See your recent form at a glance

The dedicated Insights tab gives you a clearer picture of how your recent matches are going. Instead of opening results one by one, you can see form and performance patterns across the matches PadelWrist has recorded.

Track performance over time

Trends make it easier to see whether results are changing rather than judging yourself on one good or bad session. You can compare performance across time and scoring formats using the match data already in your history.

Understand who you play well with

Saved players become much more useful when they connect to your results. Explore your record with regular partners, see how often you have played together and compare the outcomes of those matches.

The same idea applies to opponents, giving you a practical head-to-head view of the people you face most often.

Post-match insights surface milestones and streaks

Version 1.4 can highlight notable form, milestones and winning streaks after a match. These are based on the information PadelWrist genuinely has rather than invented shot or tactical data.

PadelWrist knows points, match state, players, formats and results. It does not pretend to know whether a point came from a smash, forced error or positioning decision unless you recorded that information yourself.

Add context to the numbers

Match details such as match type, your playing side and private notes help explain why two apparently similar results may have felt very different. That context stays attached to the match so your history remains useful later.

Your data, not a public leaderboard

PadelWrist does not require a public player profile to make Insights useful. Match history and saved players can sync privately through iCloud, while the app remains focused on your own matches rather than creating a social ranking network.

Build your Insights by scoring real matches.

Score from Apple Watch, then open Insights on iPhone or iPad to see what your history is starting to tell you.
