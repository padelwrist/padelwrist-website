Padel match history

Keep the result after the match.

PadelWrist keeps a simple local history of your matches so you can review what happened, correct a saved result or resume a match you did not finish.

A match history without an account

You do not need to create a PadelWrist account just to remember a score. Match history is stored locally on your devices and can be viewed from iPhone or iPad.

This keeps the history useful without turning a simple scoring app into a social network or profile system.

Review completed matches

After a match finishes, you can return to the history and see the saved result. That makes it easier to remember previous games without keeping screenshots or notes elsewhere.

Edit a result when something was entered incorrectly

If a historic set score needs correcting, PadelWrist lets you edit the saved result. The match outcome updates from the edited sets rather than treating the original result as permanent.

This is particularly useful when a scoring mistake is noticed after the match has ended.

Resume unfinished matches

An unfinished match does not have to disappear. PadelWrist can preserve the current state so you can reopen the match and continue from the score that was already recorded.

Clear history when you want to

History belongs to you. PadelWrist includes controls to remove individual matches and clear stored history when it is no longer useful.

History is becoming Player Intelligence

The current release keeps history deliberately practical: saved results, correction, resuming unfinished matches and local control of your data. Version 1.3 builds on that foundation with richer Player Intelligence on iPhone and iPad.

Player Intelligence includes performance trends, partner and opponent analysis, head-to-head views, point timelines, momentum and stronger filtering where the information can be derived accurately from recorded match data. The aim is useful context, not invented statistics.

Frequently asked questions

Can I view padel match history on Apple Watch?

The main history and editing experience is designed for iPhone and iPad. Apple Watch is focused on fast in-match scoring.

Can I delete old matches?

Yes. You can remove saved matches and clear history.

Do I need an account to save history?

No. PadelWrist does not require an account.
