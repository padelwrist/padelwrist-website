Available now · PadelWrist 1.3

Share the match without building a social network around it.

PadelWrist sharing is designed around the useful moments: showing someone the final result, creating a polished match recap and letting another person follow a live score when that genuinely helps.

Polished result cards

A good share card should be understandable before someone opens the app. Version 1.3 includes clearer visual match summaries that can show the result, teams and relevant match context in a format that is easy to send through the normal Apple Share Sheet.

Basic result sharing is part of the core product direction rather than something that should require a public PadelWrist profile.

Live match sharing

Version 1.3 also includes host and join flows for live match sharing. The goal is simple: one person can host the live match state and another can follow it when there is a real reason to do so, without turning the scoring experience into a chat feed or public social timeline.

The live scoring device still owns the match interaction. Sharing should observe the score rather than introduce a second source of truth that could compromise scoring reliability.

Sharing should not interrupt play

Apple Watch remains the primary scoring surface, and the next scoring action must stay immediate. Hosting or sharing a match should therefore be configured around the match rather than creating extra steps after every point.

On iPhone and iPad, the larger interface can handle setup, joining, result presentation and post-match sharing more comfortably.

No account required for the core experience

PadelWrist is intentionally not becoming a public player network. The current product does not require an account for scoring, and the sharing experience is built so a useful result or recap does not depend on building a social profile first.
