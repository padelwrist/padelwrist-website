Made for people who bring players together

A better experience for hosts and players.

Club nights work best when the organiser isn't tied to a spreadsheet and every player understands the plan. PadelWrist 2.0 brings them into a single native organiser workflow.

01 · Save organiser time

Set up once. Keep the evening moving.

Bring the participant list, courts, points target and format into one place. Spend less time rebuilding rounds or passing around a draw.

02 · Give players clarity

Know your partner, opponents and court.

Make each round easy to follow, including who is on court and who's sitting out. Published read-only event views give participants the details without organiser permissions.

Choose the kind of session you want.

Use Americano for planned rotations or Mexicano for standings-informed pairings. Both aim to make social play simpler to run.

Two social formats

Same courts. Different logic.

Both formats can use short, fixed-total rally-point matches, but the way players are paired changes the character of the event.

Americano

Mix the players.

Typically uses planned rotations so players meet different partners and opponents throughout the session. Individual standings add up each player's credited team points.

Events questions

What organisers and players need to know.

Is PadelWrist Events available?

Yes. PadelWrist 2.0 includes Americano and Mexicano Events for organisers on iPhone and iPad. Participants follow published fixtures and standings in read-only mode.

Which devices can organise a tournament?

The organiser manages Events on iPhone or iPad. Apple Watch continues to support individual match scoring and related features; it is not the tournament organiser.

What happens in Americano and Mexicano rounds?

Americano proposes planned rotations. Mexicano uses confirmed individual standings to propose new pairings in later rounds. The organiser reviews and publishes the next draw.

Can participants follow their next court?

Yes. Version 2.0 includes a read-only following view accessed by event code or link for published matches, court assignments, results and standings.

Does Events take bookings or entry payments?

No. Events helps run the sport itself, not court booking, ticketing or payment collection.
