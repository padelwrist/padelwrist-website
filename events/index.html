The proposed organiser journey

One place to run the session.

Rather than spreading the draw, court assignments and standings across a spreadsheet and a group chat, the goal is to keep every stage of a social event understandable on one screen.

  1. 01

    Set it up

    Choose Americano or Mexicano, add players and courts, set a total-points target and decide how long you want to play.

  2. 02

    Review the draw

    Inspect proposed pairings and court assignments before publishing. Keep off-court players and rests visible.

  3. 03

    Confirm results

    An authorised organiser approves official scores. The event's standings come from confirmed results rather than unverified entries.

  4. 04

    Run another round

    Review current standings, generate the next fixtures when you are ready, or finish the session when time runs out.

These steps describe the target product experience, not currently available features.

Two social formats

Same courts. Different logic.

Both formats can use short, fixed-total rally-point matches, but the way players are paired changes the character of the event.

Americano

Mix the players.

Typically uses planned rotations so players meet different partners and opponents throughout the session. Individual standings add up each player's credited team points.

Official scores, with accountability

A correction should never be invisible.

The latest draft work supports current and historic fixture corrections. The organiser enters a reason, reviews the original score against the replacement and confirms the change explicitly.

Server-led revisions and audit records help prevent duplicate changes after a connection loss and reject an edit when another organiser has already changed the result. Previously published fixtures remain stable rather than automatically reshuffling.

Development status: this behaviour has been tested in development builds, but is not merged or released. Accessibility, real-device and broader product acceptance are still required.

Events questions

The important distinctions.

Is PadelWrist Events available?

Not yet. PadelWrist Events is in development. The released PadelWrist app currently supports scoring individual Standard and Fixed Points matches but does not manage full tournament draws and standings.

Will Events be an iPhone and iPad feature?

That is the current product direction. Organisers will manage events from the native PadelWrist iPhone or iPad app. Apple Watch tournament administration is not part of the proposed initial scope.

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

Americano typically follows pre-planned rotating partnerships. Mexicano uses confirmed results and standings to propose later-round pairings. The organiser reviews proposals before a new round is published.

Will organisers be able to correct an earlier result?

Audited correction flows for current and historic official results are being developed. The current draft work requires a reason, an explicit review of old and replacement scores, and server confirmation. These are not released Events features.

Does Events include bookings or entry fee collection?

No court booking or event-fee collection is included in the proposed initial product. Pricing and release date have not been announced.
