Made for people who bring players together

A better experience for hosts and players.

Club nights work best when the organiser isn't tied to a spreadsheet and every player understands the plan. This is what we're designing PadelWrist Events to make easier.

01 · Save organiser time

Set up once. Keep the evening moving.

Bring the participant list, courts, points target and format into one place. Spend less time rebuilding rounds or passing around a draw.

02 · Give players clarity

Know your partner, opponents and court.

Make each round easy to follow, including who is on court and who's sitting out. Optional read-only guest views are part of the concept.

03 · Make it sociable

Choose the kind of session you want.

Use Americano for planned rotations or Mexicano for standings-informed pairings. Both aim to make social play simpler to run.

04 · Finish with confidence

See how the whole event played out.

Proposed individual standings and organiser-confirmed scores help everyone understand the results without chasing paper scorecards.

All Events organiser capabilities above remain in development. Individual match scoring is available today through PadelWrist.

From setup to final standings

One app for the whole club session.

A proposed four-step flow inside PadelWrist on iPhone and iPad. The organiser stays in charge of each round, while the players stay focused on padel.

  1. 01

    Create a social

    Name the event, select the format and enter players, available courts and a points target.

  2. 02

    Share the draw

    Review pairings and court assignments before publishing the next round to players.

  3. 03

    Run your rounds

    Keep results in one place, see how the standings change and decide when to start again.

  4. 04

    Wrap it up

    Finish when court time runs out and give your group a clear picture of the final standings.

This is a product preview, not a live event organiser or a promise that every proposed workflow will ship unchanged.

Two social formats

Same courts. Different logic.

Both formats can use short, fixed-total rally-point matches, but the way players are paired changes the character of the event.

Americano

Mix the players.

Typically uses planned rotations so players meet different partners and opponents throughout the session. Individual standings add up each player's credited team points.

Events questions

The important distinctions.

Is PadelWrist Events available?

Not yet. PadelWrist Events is in development. The released PadelWrist app currently supports scoring individual Standard and Fixed Points matches but does not manage full tournament draws and standings.

Will Events be an iPhone and iPad feature?

That is the current product direction. Organisers will manage events from the native PadelWrist iPhone or iPad app. Apple Watch tournament administration is not part of the proposed initial scope.

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

Americano typically follows pre-planned rotating partnerships. Mexicano uses confirmed results and standings to propose later-round pairings. The organiser reviews proposals before a new round is published.

Will players see who they are playing and which court?

That is an important goal for PadelWrist Events. Proposed published round views show partners, opponents, courts and off-court players. Optional read-only participant information is under consideration, not yet publicly available.

Does Events include bookings or entry fee collection?

No court booking or event-fee collection is included in the proposed initial product. Pricing and release date have not been announced.
