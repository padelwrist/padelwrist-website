# Padel vs tennis.

They share a net and a familiar score, but the moment the ball reaches the glass they become very different sports.

## The short version

Padel is played on a smaller enclosed court, normally as doubles, with a solid racket and an underarm serve. Tennis uses a larger open court, strung rackets and an overarm serve. Padel's walls remain part of the rally after the ball bounces.

## Court

A standard padel court is 20m by 10m and enclosed by glass and metal fencing. A tennis court is larger and open. The smaller footprint changes movement: padel is less about covering huge distances and more about moving as a pair and controlling the net.

## Racket and ball

A padel racket is solid, stringless and perforated. It has a shorter handle and compact hitting surface. Padel balls look similar to tennis balls but are specified for padel and typically have lower internal pressure.

## Serve

Tennis begins with an overarm serve. Padel begins underarm: bounce the ball, strike it below waist height and send it diagonally into the service box. That makes the first few rallies much more accessible for newcomers.

## Walls

This is the biggest difference. In padel, a ball that bounces in your court can then rebound from the glass or fence and still be returned. It rewards patience and makes defensive situations recoverable. Read the [ wall rules ](https://padelwrist.com/padel-wall-rules/) before your first game.

## Scoring

The traditional structure is familiar: love, 15, 30, 40, games and sets. Deuce systems can differ, particularly in competitions, so see our [ 2026 padel scoring guide ](https://padelwrist.com/how-padel-scoring-works/) .

## Tactics

Tennis players often arrive with enough racket skill to strike the ball well, then discover that padel punishes the wrong positional habits. Staying glued to the baseline, trying to pass opponents from difficult positions and smashing every overhead are common traps.

In padel, the pair controlling the net usually has the attacking position. The lob is therefore a fundamental tactical shot because it can push opponents back and let you take the net.

## Which is easier?

Padel is generally easier to * start * . The underarm serve and compact court help beginners get a rally going quickly. That should not be confused with being easy to master: defending glass, moving with a partner, choosing overheads and controlling tempo all take practice.
