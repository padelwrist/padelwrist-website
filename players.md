## Set up. Score. Understand.

Your devices have different jobs. PadelWrist keeps scoring fast on court and leaves the deeper information for after the match.

- **1. Set up your match**

Pick players and rules on iPhone or iPad, or set up a supported match on your Watch.

- **2. Keep playing**

Record points on Apple Watch, follow the serving order and keep the match moving.

- **3. Use your results**

Save the match, check History and Insights, or choose Play Again for another game.

## Player questions.

Can I score padel matches on Apple Watch?

Yes. PadelWrist supports Standard Match and Fixed Points scoring on Apple Watch, with a companion experience on iPhone and iPad.

Will PadelWrist save my match history and stats?

Yes. Saved matches feed searchable History and Insights, including recent form, partner and opponent records and performance trends based on recorded results.

Can I repeat a match with the same players and settings?

Yes. PadelWrist 1.9 adds Play Again from Match History on iPhone and iPad. Review the previous setup and start a separate new match, or prepare for Watch from iPhone.

Can clubs use PadelWrist for tournaments?

PadelWrist 2.0 introduces Americano and Mexicano Events for clubs and tournament hosts on iPhone and iPad. Events is part of PadelWrist 2.0. [Explore Clubs](https://padelwrist.com/clubs/) .
