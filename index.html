Why PADELWRIST

The score is the interface.

During play, the important information stays obvious: point score, games, sets, server and the match state. Away from the court, iPhone and iPad give you the space to set up matches, review history and manage your game.

01 · Apple Watch

Score from your wrist

Large, fast scoring controls keep the point score, server and important states visible without pulling you away from the rally.

02 · One live match

Move between devices

Prepare on iPhone or iPad, start on Apple Watch, or open a Watch-started match on iPhone. The match remains one shared state.

03 · Match history

Keep the result after the court

Save player names, review set-by-set results, resume unfinished matches and correct historic scores when needed.
