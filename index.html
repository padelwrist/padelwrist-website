Why PADELWRIST

The score stays with you, not with a device.

One active match can move with you across your Apple devices, with wrist-first scoring designed to keep your attention on the court.

01 · Wrist first

Score from your wrist

Add points from Apple Watch in seconds, with large controls and spoken score announcements so you can stay focused on the game.

02 · One match

Start anywhere. Continue anywhere.

Prepare on iPhone or iPad, start on Apple Watch, or join an active match from another device. The live score stays together.

03 · Reliable

Keep scoring even when devices disconnect

Scoring is local-first, so a temporary connection drop does not stop the match. Devices reconcile again when they reconnect.
