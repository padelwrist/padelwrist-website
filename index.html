Why PadelWrist

The score should never become the distraction.

PadelWrist is built around the moment you are actually playing: the score, server and match state stay obvious, while setup and history move to the bigger screens when you need them.

01

Score from your wrist

Apple Watch keeps the point score, server and important match states close without making you reach for your phone after every rally.

02

Move between devices

Prepare on iPhone or iPad, start on Apple Watch, or open a Watch-started match on iPhone. It stays one logical match rather than separate copies.

03

Keep the result afterwards

Review set-by-set results, reuse saved players, resume unfinished matches and correct historic scores when something needs fixing later.

Everything in the first release

Built for the whole match.

The feature set stays focused on practical scoring rather than turning PadelWrist into a tournament manager, social network or account system.

Scoring

  • Standard padel scoring with games and sets
  • Best-of-three match logic and tie-breaks
  • Fixed Points for point-based match formats
  • Game point, set point and match point states

During play

  • Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad scoring
  • Server tracking and spoken score announcements
  • Undo and quick score corrections
  • Local-first scoring during temporary disconnection

Across devices

  • Prepare a match on iPhone or iPad for Apple Watch
  • Open a Watch-started active match on iPhone
  • Keep the active match aligned when connectivity is available

After the match

  • Match history on iPhone and iPad
  • Saved players and teams
  • Resume unfinished matches
  • Edit historic results
  • No account required

Designed for each screen

One PadelWrist.
Three different jobs.

The visual language stays consistent, but each Apple device is used for what it is actually good at rather than stretching one interface everywhere.

Apple WatchPlay the match. Score quickly with the most important state always visible.
iPhoneSet up, score, resume, review history and manage your padel matches.
iPadManage matches on a larger canvas or use it as a glanceable court-side scoreboard.
