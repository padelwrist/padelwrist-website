Common questions

PadelWrist, explained simply.

The essentials about scoring, devices and what PadelWrist is designed to do.

What is PadelWrist?

PadelWrist is a padel scoring and match-tracking app for Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad. Score from your wrist, keep the live match visible on iPhone, then use Match History and Insights to understand your results afterwards.

Can I keep score in padel on Apple Watch?

Yes. Apple Watch is designed for quick in-match scoring, with the point score, server, important match states and spoken score announcements close at hand.

Does PadelWrist work if my devices temporarily disconnect?

Yes. Active scoring is local-first, so temporary connection loss does not stop the scoring device. Supported devices reconcile the current match again when connectivity returns.

Do I need a PadelWrist account?

No. You can score matches, use Match History and access the core PadelWrist experience without creating a PadelWrist account. Match history and saved players can sync privately through iCloud.

What is Fixed Points scoring?

Fixed Points counts every rally as one point instead of using love, 15, 30 and 40. It is useful for point-based social match formats.

What does PadelWrist 1.4 add?

Version 1.4 adds a dedicated Insights tab, recent form and player analysis, stronger Match History search and filters, match details and private notes, post-match milestones and streaks, plus Live Activities and Dynamic Island support on iPhone.
